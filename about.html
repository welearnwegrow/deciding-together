About this guide

A field guide to making decisions together maps the ways groups make decisions, and the social forces that shape how people reach agreement inside each one. It sorts 22 structures and methods into five groups: shaping proposals, deciding by authority, by vote or by consensus, and cases where no one is in charge. Many come with variations, which are described inside them. This education edition uses examples from schools, school systems and education projects, with a few from the wider social impact sector.

It is written for students, teachers, parents, school leaders, policy makers, and the NGOs and universities that work with schools: anyone who takes part in decisions about education. It builds on two earlier articles: How to collaborate? (2019), which first compared hierarchy, no structure, majority, unanimity, consensus and consent, and A better way to dialogue (2025), which looked at twelve dialogue traditions schools can learn from.
