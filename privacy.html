Privacy policy

Last updated: 21 November 2026

This policy explains what information Deciding Together: A Field Guide (welearnwegrow.github.io/deciding-together) collects about readers, why, who processes it for us, how long we keep it and what rights you have. The guide is made by We Learn, We Grow, which is responsible for this information.

Contact for anything in this policy, including requests to see or delete your data: [CONTACT EMAIL].

What we collect and why

WhatWhenWhy
Your email address, and whether you asked for updatesOnly when you download a PDFTo provide the download and to understand who uses the guide. If you tick the box, to send you occasional updates about the guide.
Anonymous usage records: a random ID kept in your browser's local storage, the pages you visit, the structures, social forces and methods you open, how far you scroll and how long a page is visible, the name of the site that linked to us (not the full address), the type of device (worked out from screen width) and your countryWhen you use the guide, unless your browser sends Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control, in which case none of this is recordedTo see which parts of the guide are useful and improve it. The random ID is not linked to your name or email. Your country comes from your internet address, but we do not store the address itself.
Feedback: your reaction to an example and any note you addOnly when you press a feedback buttonTo improve the examples
Guide me: the decision you describe and the answersWhen you use Guide meTo give you an answer. The text is sent to our AI provider to produce it. We keep the conversation only if you tick the box agreeing to it; we keep an estimate of the cost of every conversation.

Legal basis

  • Updates by email: your consent, which you can withdraw at any time.
  • Download email, usage records and feedback: our legitimate interest in providing the guide and understanding how it is used, kept to the minimum needed.
  • Saved Guide me conversations: your consent.

Who processes it for us

  • Cloudflare runs the small service that receives the records and forms above.
  • Notion stores them.
  • Anthropic produces the Guide me answers.
  • GitHub hosts the website and, like any web host, receives your internet address when pages load.
  • Google serves the fonts and runs Google Tag Manager on the pages.

Some of these companies are based in the United States, so your information may be processed there. We do not sell or share your information with anyone else, and we do not use it for advertising.

How long we keep it

  • Email addresses: until you ask us to delete yours or unsubscribe from updates, and in any case no longer than 24 months after your last download.
  • Usage records, feedback and saved Guide me conversations: up to 24 months.

Your rights

You can ask to see the information we hold about you, correct it, delete it, object to how we use it, or withdraw consent for updates. Write to the contact address above. Because usage records are anonymous, we can only find yours if you tell us the random ID in your browser. You can also complain to your data protection authority; in the Netherlands, that is the Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens.

Students

If you are under 16, please ask a parent, carer or teacher to download the PDF for you rather than entering your own email address.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page.
