Privacy policy

Last updated: 22 November 2026

This site is run by We Learn, We Grow. Contact us through the form at welearnwegrow.bio.

What we collect and why

  • Email address, and whether you ask for updates, when you download a PDF: to provide the download and, only if you tick the box, to send updates. You need to give an email to download; updates are optional. Basis: legitimate interest; updates with your consent.
  • Anonymous usage data (pages and items opened, scrolling, linking site, device type, country), linked to a random ID in your browser, not to you: to improve the guide. Nothing is recorded if your browser sends Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control. Basis: legitimate interest.
  • Feedback and Guide me text you choose to send: to improve the guide and answer your question. Guide me conversations are kept only if you tick the box. Basis: legitimate interest, or your consent where you tick a box.

Cookies and storage

We set no cookies, and Google Analytics runs without cookies. Your browser stores a random ID and a note of what was already counted; you can clear these at any time.

Who processes it

Cloudflare, Notion, Anthropic (Guide me), GitHub (hosting) and Google (fonts and analytics). Some are in the United States. We never sell your data.

How long we keep it

Up to 24 months, or until you ask us to delete it or unsubscribe.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct, delete or receive a copy of your data, object to its use, or withdraw consent at any time, through the contact form. You can also complain to your data protection authority (in the Netherlands, the Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens).

Students

If you are under 16, please ask a parent, carer or teacher to download the PDF for you.
