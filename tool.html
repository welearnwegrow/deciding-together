Guide me

Describe a decision you are facing, in any organisation or field. Guide me suggests how to make it: a sequence of steps using structures and methods from this guide, and the social forces to call out. Treat it as a starting point to talk through with the people the decision affects.

Say what has to be decided and what has happened so far. The questions alongside will help you cover what matters. A fuller description gives a sharper answer.

Things to consider

Use these to frame your description. Cover the ones that apply.

  1. What is your role, and the goal behind the decision?Who you are in this decision, what it is meant to achieve, and whether the people involved share that goal.
  2. Who is affected?How many people, and which groups.
  3. How complex is it?Is there a known answer, one experts could work out, or one no one can know in advance? Or is something going wrong right now?
  4. Who needs to accept it for it to work?Only the decider, the people carrying it out, or everyone affected?
  5. Is disagreement likely?About what, and between whom?
  6. Is time short?Is there a deadline, and what happens if it's missed?
  7. Can you undo it cheaply?What would it cost to change course later?
  8. Could a few people dominate?Who holds power, speaks first or controls information?
